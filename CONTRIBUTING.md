# Contributing to Mo-Vistas

Thanks for helping make Odisha's lesser-known destinations easier to discover.

## Before proposing a destination

- Verify facts with reliable, current sources and/or local community partners.
- Confirm that publicising the location is appropriate, especially for ecologically or culturally sensitive places.
- Do not publish private phone numbers, personal information, or photos without permission.
- Include when information was checked and note seasonal or access restrictions.
- Clearly distinguish confirmed facts from tips or personal recommendations.

## How to contribute

1. Fork the repository.
2. Create a branch for your change.
3. Keep edits focused and test the site on a phone-sized and desktop-sized viewport.
4. Open a pull request explaining what changed and how destination information was verified.

Please be respectful, accurate and community-minded.
